<p>We are happy to announce that the <strong>QGIS User Conference 2026</strong> will take place on <strong>5–6 October 2026</strong> in <strong>Laax</strong>, in the heart of the Swiss Alps. Visit the <a href="https://conference.qgis.org/">conference website</a> to find out all details.</p>



<figure><a href="hero.webp"><img loading="lazy" width="1024" height="574" src="hero.webp" alt="" srcset="hero.webp 1024w, hero.webp 150w, hero.webp 300w, hero.webp 768w, hero.webp 1440w, hero.webp 1532w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></a></figure>



<p>The conference will be hosted at <strong>Crap Sogn Gion</strong>, at <strong>2,222 metres above sea level</strong>, offering a unique setting with panoramic mountain views and direct access to the surrounding alpine landscape. Despite its mountain location, Laax is well connected by public transport and provides a wide range of accommodation options in the valley.</p>



<p>As Chair of the <strong>QGIS Project</strong> and of the QGIS User Conference 2026, I’m very much looking forward to welcoming the community in my hometown and place of birth of QField.</p>



<figure><a href="venue_2.webp"><img loading="lazy" width="1024" height="682" src="venue_2.webp" alt="" srcset="venue_2.webp 1024w, venue_2.webp 150w, venue_2.webp 300w, venue_2.webp 768w, venue_2.webp 1440w, venue_2.webp 1920w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></a></figure>



<p>The conference will bring together users, developers, and contributors from around the world for two days of presentations, workshops, and discussions covering a wide range of QGIS-related topics. Around the main conference days, additional events are planned, including workshops, community activities, and the QGIS Contributor Meeting.</p>



<figure><a href="attending_2.webp"><img loading="lazy" width="1024" height="682" src="attending_2.webp" alt="" srcset="attending_2.webp 1024w, attending_2.webp 150w, attending_2.webp 300w, attending_2.webp 768w, attending_2.webp 1200w" sizes="(max-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1024px" /></a></figure>



<p>More information about the program, tickets, and related events will be published over the coming months on the conference website <strong><a href="https://conference.qgis.org/">https://conference.qgis.org/</a></strong></p>



<p>We are looking forward to welcoming you to Laax in October 2026.<br>Cheers Marco</p>
