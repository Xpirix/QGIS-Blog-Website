<pre>Lutra Consulting, North Road and Hobu are collaborating in a new crowd-funding campaign to extend these capabilities in future QGIS releases!</pre>



<figure><a href="elevation_profile.jpg"><img loading="lazy" width="500" height="354" src="elevation_profile.jpg" alt="" srcset="elevation_profile.jpg 500w, elevation_profile.jpg 150w, elevation_profile.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 500px) 100vw, 500px" /></a></figure>



<p>Highlights of the planned improvements include: </p>



<ul>
<li><strong>Point Clouds</strong><br>Creating point cloud processing tools for transformation, management and analysis of point clouds. Ensuring that extremely large (terabyte size) datasets can be handled well for both display and analysis.</li>



<li><strong>Elevation Profiles</strong><br>Support embedding customisable elevation profiles into print layouts and atlases, and allow exporting elevation profiles to CSV and DXF.</li>



<li><strong>3D Maps</strong><br>Faster 3D maps for large scenes, an improved 3D measurement tool and further improvements to 3D scene navigation.</li>
</ul>



<p>Your financial support is vital to make these improvements possible! Visit the <a href="https://www.lutraconsulting.co.uk/crowdfunding/pointcloud-processing-qgis/">crowd funding page</a> for additional information on what is included in the effort and how you can financially contribute.</p>
