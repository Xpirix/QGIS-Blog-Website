<p>In January, we wanted to learn more about if and how QGIS users contribute back to the project. We received 299 responses from all over the world:</p>
<p><a href="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png"><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png" alt="user_survey_january_map" width="978" height="491" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png 978w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png 300w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_map.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 978px) 100vw, 978px" /></a></p>
<p>55% of responders have contributed to the project in the past:</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_1.png" alt="user_survey_january_1" width="618" height="118" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_1.png 618w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_1.png 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_1.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 618px) 100vw, 618px" /></p>
<p>Responders who stated that they had contributed to QGIS were asked to specify what kind of contributions they had provided. This question was multiple-choice. Time contributions are generally more common than financial contributions. 30% of responders helped by creating reproducible bug reports and 24% implemented improvements themselves. The most common financial contribution are personal donations to QGIS.ORG at 17%.</p>
<p>Membership in user groups, contracting developers / documentation writers / translators, or having a support contract with a QGIS support provider are less common amongst responders:</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_2.png" alt="user_survey_january_2" width="618" height="621" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_2.png 618w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_2.png 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_2.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 618px) 100vw, 618px" /></p>
<p>Responders who stated that they had not contributed to QGIS most commonly stated that they didn&#8217;t know how to contribute (26%), while lacking financial resources were only raised by 10% of responders:</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_3.png" alt="user_survey_january_3" width="619" height="183" srcset="/wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_3.png 619w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_3.png 150w, /wp-content/uploads/2019/02/user_survey_january_3.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 619px) 100vw, 619px" /></p>
