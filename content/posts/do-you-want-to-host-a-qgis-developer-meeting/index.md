<p>Each year the QGIS.ORG community holds two developer meetings. These events are an important part of  our project &#8211; they provide an invaluable opportunity for us all to meet face to face and share ideas, discuss issues and plan the future of QGIS.</p>
<p>The host of the developer meeting gets a special bonus for hosting the meeting: One of our releases will be named after the town / village / city etc. where the event was held &#8211; like this:</p>
<p><img loading="lazy" src="screen-shot-2017-09-02-at-11-11-31-pm.png" alt="Screen Shot 2017-09-02 at 11.11.31 PM.png" width="902" height="430" srcset="screen-shot-2017-09-02-at-11-11-31-pm.png 902w, screen-shot-2017-09-02-at-11-11-31-pm.png 150w, screen-shot-2017-09-02-at-11-11-31-pm.png 300w, screen-shot-2017-09-02-at-11-11-31-pm.png 768w" sizes="(max-width: 902px) 100vw, 902px" /></p>
<p>We want to have a better idea of which venues we will be using for future events to help with out planning. So I am putting out a call for venue proposals:</p>
<blockquote><p><strong>If you would like to host a QGIS developer meeting (estimated 50 people per event) or a QGIS Conference (estimated 100-150 people per event) please contact us!</strong></p></blockquote>
<p>Please don&#8217;t submit proposals unless you have the authority to make such a proposal and are willing to act as the local organiser for the event. To make a proposal, fill out <a href="https://goo.gl/forms/hIqXbq0cLdYfiC832">this form</a> and tell us about your great venue!</p>
